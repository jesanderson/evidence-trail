TEACHER EXEMPLARS · GUIDANCE BEFORE YOU WRITE

Three language pathways. One strong explanation.

Shared target: Explain how Athene and Hermes prepare Perseus. Support your answer with one accurate detail for each helper, locate the evidence, and explain how each contribution helps.

These are complete teacher-written models, not hints generated from your response. Choose the language support that helps you communicate your thinking. Short, clear sentences can show strong reasoning.

PATHWAY A · MORE LANGUAGE SUPPORT

Build with clear sentences

C · Claim
Athene and Hermes help Perseus face danger and travel.

E · Evidence
First, Athene gives Perseus a polished shield. She tells him to look at Medusa’s image in the shield, not at Medusa herself. Next, Hermes gives him winged sandals. The sandals carry him across the seas. These details are in the opening conversation before Perseus leaves the cliff.

R · Reasoning
The shield helps Perseus see Medusa safely. He can avoid her eyes. Her eyes can turn him to stone. The sandals help him cross the sea without a ship. Both gifts help him begin his journey.

Notice the language

Discourse: First / Next; repeated character and object names keep the ideas connected.
Sentence: Short, complete sentences carry the full explanation.
Word/phrase: Shield, image, and winged sandals name the evidence precisely.

Try this support: Sketch shield → safe view and sandals → travel. Point and rehearse each connection aloud or in a home language before writing.

PATHWAY B · CONNECTED LANGUAGE

Link ideas and reasons

C · Claim
Athene and Hermes prepare Perseus for danger and travel, and each helper gives him a tool for a different problem.

E · Evidence
In the opening conversation before Perseus leaves the cliff, Athene gives him a polished shield and tells him to view Medusa’s reflection in it. Hermes gives him winged sandals, and he explains that they will carry Perseus across the seas.

R · Reasoning
Perseus must not look directly at Medusa, so the shield gives him a safe way to see her. He also needs to cross the sea, but he has no ship. The sandals solve that travel problem. Together, the helpers give him a way to face the danger and reach his destination.

Notice the language

Discourse: Each helper / together connects the two contributions.
Sentence: And, so, and but join complete ideas.
Word/phrase: Face the danger and reach his destination add natural, precise phrasing.

Try this support: Say “The problem is ___. The gift helps by ___.” Join those ideas with so or but; then add the text location.

PATHWAY C · ELABORATED LANGUAGE

Explain with precise connections

C · Claim
Athene and Hermes prepare Perseus by addressing two obstacles to his quest: Medusa’s deadly gaze and the journey across the sea.

E · Evidence
During the opening conversation before Perseus leaves the cliff, Athene supplies a polished shield and instructs him to view Medusa indirectly through its reflection. Hermes provides winged sandals that will carry him across the seas.

R · Reasoning
Because looking directly at Medusa would turn Perseus to stone, Athene’s instructions make the shield a means of protection as well as a way to locate his target. While the shield addresses this immediate danger, the sandals provide transportation across water without a ship. Their contributions complement each other: one makes approaching Medusa safer, and the other makes the journey possible.

Notice the language

Discourse: This immediate danger and their contributions refer back to earlier ideas.
Sentence: Because and while connect causes and contrasting functions.
Word/phrase: Protection, transportation, and indirectly express meaning precisely.

Try this support: Ask “How do the two contributions work together?” Use because or while to make the relationship explicit, not just to lengthen the answer.

Before you write: Find both details in your copy of the text → say how each helps → write your own explanation. All three models paraphrase the source; none contains a direct quotation. Add your edition’s paragraph or page numbers after checking them.
